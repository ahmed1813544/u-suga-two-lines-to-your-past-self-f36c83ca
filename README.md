Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca).

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes one two-line text to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set). Line 1 warns the
writer's past self about one specific moment — the night of 14 October, when
Dad tosses over the car keys and says "take the car, be back by ten" — in a
single sentence. Line 2 says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what
changed because of it.

The output is fully deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Entry text

> On 14 October, when Dad tosses you the keys and says 'take the car, be back by ten,' hand them back.
>
> That drive ends with the blue Honda wrapped around the oak on Mill Road, and you spend the next three years learning to walk again instead of learning to drive.
